Uncertainty is not signalled by color alone

The unverified row carries four independent cues: a bordered container, bold weight, a question-mark glyph, and the words "not confirmed". A viewer who cannot separate the amber from the grey still reads the state.

Contrast above WCAG AA

Body text 17.0:1, secondary text 5.2:1, amber on its tinted ground 6.1:1. Dark theme holds 6.6:1 on the same pair.

Controls are labelled by action

Each chip announces what pressing it does, not just its value: "Edit constraint: party of 5". The confirm control states why it is unavailable through aria-describedby.

Processing is announced

The parsing state sits in a role="status" live region, so a screen reader hears that work is happening instead of meeting silence.

Heading order is unbroken

h1 page title, h2 section headings, h3 for the app name and each annotation, h4 inside these cards. No level is skipped and none is chosen for its size.

Keyboard and motion

Every control is reachable by Tab with a visible focus ring drawn in the accent. The loading shimmer is replaced by a static fill under prefers-reduced-motion.
